[h1]03 · Soil and carrots[/h1]

[h2]What the game unlocked[/h2]
[b]till()[/b] and [b]plant(Entities.Carrot)[/b].

[h2]The idea[/h2]
Carrots only grow on [b]tilled soil[/b]. You till a tile with [b]till()[/b]. Planting costs items: **1 Hay
and 1 Wood** per carrot, deducted when [b]plant[/b] runs.

[list]
[*]📖 Growth time: between 4.8 and 7.2 seconds.
[*]✅ An empty tile is grass; after [b]till()[/b] it becomes soil.
[/list]

[h2]The trap: [b]till()[/b] toggles[/h2]

[code]
till()   # grass -> soil
till()   # soil  -> grass  (undone!)
[/code]

Calling [b]till()[/b] on a tile that is already soil [b]turns it back into grass[/b]. Harvesting the carrot
leaves the soil tilled, so the tile does not need [b]till()[/b] again. The classic mistake is tilling on
every harvest:

[code]
# WRONG: on lap 2 the tile is already soil, till undoes it, plant fails
if can_harvest():
    harvest()
    till()
    plant(Entities.Carrot)
[/code]

Symptom: the tile keeps alternating between grass and carrot.

[h2]The fix: two phases[/h2]

[code]
# Phase 1: till and plant, once per tile
for i in range(get_world_size()):
    for j in range(get_world_size()):
        till()
        plant(Entities.Carrot)
        move(North)
    move(East)

# Phase 2: harvest and replant, no till
while True:
    for i in range(get_world_size()):
        for j in range(get_world_size()):
            if can_harvest():
                harvest()
                plant(Entities.Carrot)
            move(North)
        move(East)
[/code]

!!! warning "Running phase 1 twice breaks it"
    Because [b]till()[/b] toggles, a second run turns the already tilled tiles back into grass. ✅
    [b]clear()[/b] removes everything, moves the drone to (0,0) and turns the soil back into grass; use
    it to start over.

[h2]"Why is the harvest uneven?"[/h2]

You run phase 2 and the drone harvests tile 1, skips tiles 2 and 3, and on the next lap picks up
the ones it missed. It is not a code bug. ✅ Each carrot draws its own time between 4.8 and 7.2 s,
so some are not ready when the drone passes, and it moves on anyway. [b]move[/b] outside the [b]if[/b] does
exactly that.

⏳ You can make the drone wait on the tile with [b]while True[/b] and [b]break[/b], trading the mismatch for
idle time. We have not measured which pays more.

[h2]Economy[/h2]
Wood comes from bush; Hay comes from harvested grass. Carrots spend both. Turning the whole farm
into carrots cuts the production of what they consume, so a [b]mix[/b] (carrots on the middle tiles
and bush on the corners, or the reverse) keeps Wood coming in. ⏳ Which proportion pays most has
not been measured.

[h2]Exercise[/h2]
1. Reproduce the mistake ([b]till()[/b] inside the [b]if[/b]), watch the tile alternate, then fix it.
2. Build a carrot, bush, carrot column on side 3. Hint: the pattern fits in a single [b]for i[/b].
3. Measure: note the Wood before and after 1 minute of phase 2. What was the yield?
