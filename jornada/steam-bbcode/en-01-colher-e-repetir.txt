[h1]01 · Harvest and repeat[/h1]

[h2]What the game unlocked[/h2]
[b]while[/b], [b]if[/b]/[b]else[/b], [b]can_harvest()[/b], [b]harvest()[/b], [b]move()[/b] with [b]North[/b]/[b]East[/b]/[b]South[/b]/[b]West[/b],
[b]plant()[/b] with [b]Entities.Bush[/b].

[h2]The idea[/h2]
Your drone only runs what is in the script, and the script runs once and ends. For the farm to
work by itself, the code goes inside a [b]while True:[/b], which repeats forever.

Inside it, the question is always the same: [b]"is the plant on this tile ready?"[/b]

[code]
while True:
    if can_harvest():
        harvest()
        plant(Entities.Bush)
    move(North)
[/code]

Read it as: repeat forever; if the plant is ready, harvest and replant; [b]then[/b] move, whether
you harvested or not.

[h2]Three facts that matter[/h2]

[list]
[*]✅ [b]An empty tile is grass.[/b] [b]plant(Entities.Bush)[/b] works on it with no soil preparation.
[*]✅ [b]The border wraps around.[/b] Moving [b]North[/b] on the last row takes you to the first. On a
  side-3 farm, three steps north bring the drone back to where it started.
[*]📖 [b]An infinite loop is safe.[/b] The game puts a delay between repetitions, so [b]while True[/b]
  does not freeze anything. You stop it by pressing Execute again.
[/list]

[h2]The most common mistake: [b]move[/b] inside the [b]if[/b][/h2]

[code]
# WRONG
while True:
    if can_harvest():
        harvest()
        plant(Entities.Bush)
        move(North)      # only moves when it harvested
[/code]

If the tile is not ready, the [b]if[/b] fails, the drone [b]does not move[/b] and waits. That may be what
you want on a single tile, but on a path with several tiles it gets stuck on the first slow
plant. [b]move[/b] goes [b]outside[/b] the [b]if[/b]: the drone always moves, harvesting only where it can.

[h2]Exercise[/h2]
1. Make the drone harvest and replant bush along one column, without stopping.
2. Replace [b]plant[/b] with nothing (just [b]harvest[/b]) and watch: the tile turns back into grass by itself.
3. ⏳ Open question: on a side-3 farm, does the bush finish growing between one lap and the next?
   Count how many times [b]can_harvest()[/b] was false and check for yourself.

!!! warning "Harvesting early destroys"
    ✅ Harvesting a plant that is not ready destroys it. That is why [b]if can_harvest()[/b] is not decoration.
